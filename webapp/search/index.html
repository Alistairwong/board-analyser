To require or avoid specific holds, choose Must use, Start, Hand, Finish, Foot or Avoid above the board and tap holds. Hold size is estimated from how hard the climbs using each hold are.

New climbs are pieced together from how real climbs at that grade move between holds, then graded against the most similar real climbs.
